## Notes on the bench math

- 5 innings × 2 bench spots × 2 games = 20 bench slots. That's ~1.8 per player, so a perfectly-even "everyone sits every game" doesn't fit — 1 player has to skip sitting each game.
- I used those "no-sit" slots on your top group: Easton doesn't sit in G1, Milo doesn't sit in G1, Micah doesn't sit in G2. Every other player sits exactly once per game.
- Every position assignment sticks to each kid's 1st, 2nd, or 3rd listed preference.
