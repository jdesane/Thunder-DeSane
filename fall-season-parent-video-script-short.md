## 6. THE THREE THINGS TO KNOW ABOUT THIS SEASON (75 sec)

Three things I need every family to hear:

**One — this is a transition year to kid pitch.** The game is going to be slower and messier, especially early. There will be walks. There will be rough innings. That's normal. **I need patience** — from parents, from players, from all of us.

**Two — players will sit more this season than before.** We're on strict pitch counts and we're tracking everything at a high level. Our rule of thumb for pitching: **a player has to throw at least 50% strikes in practice** to earn the mound in a game. Pitching gets earned through practice, mechanics, control, and the ability to handle it mentally.

**Three — effort is the standard.** We don't expect perfection. **We expect effort.** Hustle on and off the field, run everything out, no throwing helmets, no shutting down after a bad at-bat, no blaming umpires or teammates. Errors are baseball. Attitude is a choice.

---

## 7. PARENTS IN THE STANDS (45 sec)

Sideline rules, quick and clear:

- **Cheer for our team, not against the other team.** Positive only.
- **No yelling instructions during at-bats or on defense.** One voice coaches the player — that's the coach.
- **No arguing balls and strikes** with umpires. We'll handle legit issues.
- **No comments about other kids, other teams, or other families** where kids can hear them.

Our kids need to see adults model the sportsmanship we're asking of them.

---

## 8. TALKING WITH COACHES (30 sec)

We're always happy to talk about **your player** — development, what he needs to improve, how you can help at home, what his strengths are.

What we won't do is emotional, in-the-moment playing-time discussions, or conversations about other kids' playing time, position, or ability. Give it some time, then come talk — we'll keep it productive.

---

## 9. ATTENDANCE (20 sec)

**Update availability as early as possible.** Lineups, pitching plans, and rotations depend on it. Baseball is a team commitment — please treat games and tournaments that way whenever you reasonably can.

---

## 10. CLOSE (20 sec)

Success this fall isn't just wins and losses. It's watching these kids handle the transition, hustle, and pick each other up.

**We're going to coach them hard, support them harder, and give them every chance to become better players and better teammates.**

Thanks for trusting us with your kid. **Go Thunder.**
